# Layers: Zero-Budget Marketing Campaign

A real, measured marketing campaign for **layers**, an AI life-coaching / journaling app, run as a live startup challenge with the goal of driving paid subscriptions on **no budget**.

Team project for the **Hult Live Challenge (Marketing)**, Fall 2025.

---

## The problem

A real early-stage startup needs paid subscribers, not just downloads, and has no marketing budget. Each team got a unique tracking link and QR code; the only goal that counted was conversions. This forced a precise, low-cost, high-impact approach.

## The approach (three phases)

1. **User research (Phase 1):** every team member used the app via voice and text, then documented onboarding, core flows, points of delight, and friction.
2. **Strategy (Phase 2):** defined two Gen-Z personas, landed on the core insight that *people see their problems but not the patterns behind them*, and built around emotional, curiosity-triggering messaging under the slogan **"Make sense of your life."** Channels chosen for zero spend: emotional micro-influencers, word of mouth, and posters.
3. **Pilot & analytics (Phase 3):** shipped the campaign and tracked the funnel.

## Results

| Funnel stage | Count |
|---|---|
| Visits / interactions | 15 |
| Installs | 15 |
| Trials | 4 |
| Subscriptions | 0 (expected at pilot stage) |

**Bottom line:** word-of-mouth + texting drove **99% of installs** at a **~68% conversion** rate, while posters underperformed (weak call-to-action, low visibility). The takeaway: for an emotional product, trust-based human channels work best — double down on peer ambassadors and redesign passive channels rather than dropping them.
